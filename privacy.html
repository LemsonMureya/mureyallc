Website privacy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This notice covers Mureya LLC’s company website. FarmnFresh has its own app privacy policy.

When you browse

This is an informational website. We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact forms, or account sign-in. The site does not set its own cookies or store information in your browser.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. Hosting and network providers may process information needed to deliver and protect the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

When you contact us

If you email us, Mureya LLC receives your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond, provide support, and manage the inquiry. Our email service provider also processes the message. Please do not email passwords or other sensitive account credentials.

We retain correspondence as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request deletion or ask about information you have shared.

Links to our products

Links to FarmnFresh’s support, privacy, and account-deletion pages open a separate website hosted by Google Sites. Those pages and the FarmnFresh app have their own data practices. This website notice does not replace the app’s privacy policy.
